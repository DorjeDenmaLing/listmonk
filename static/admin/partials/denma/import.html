Tags

Give tags in the attributes, as a list: "{""tags"": [""volunteer"", ""donor""]}". A tag the center doesn't have is left out, unless you choose to add it in the preview.

Consent records

With "Keep the consent dates in the file", give when and how each person agreed in the attributes: "{""consent_at"": ""2019-03-04 15:22:10"", ""consent_source"": ""Mailchimp sign-up form""}". A date without a time zone is read as UTC. Other columns, like Mailchimp's OPTIN_TIME, aren't read.
