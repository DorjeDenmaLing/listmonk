Sign-up webhooks

How websites and other systems sign people up. A Form webhook is for a form on the center's website: people confirm by e-mail before they're subscribed. A Server webhook is for a system that already has their consent, such as a registration form's newsletter box; only superadmins make them.

Setting up a form

Gravity Forms (WordPress, with its Webhooks add-on): in the form's Settings → Webhooks, add one with the Form webhook's address as the Request URL, method POST, format JSON and body Select Fields, with these keys:

  • email: the e-mail field (required).
  • name, or first_name and last_name: the name fields (optional).
  • consent: the consent checkbox, if the form has one (optional).
  • form_id, entry_id, page_url: kept with their consent record (optional).
  • attribs.<name>: anything else to keep, such as attribs.city (optional).
  • tags: tags to give, separated by commas (optional).

Turn on the form's spam protection (a Turnstile or reCAPTCHA field): otherwise bots can make the center e-mail strangers.
