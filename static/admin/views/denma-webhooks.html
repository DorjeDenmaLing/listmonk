Sign-up webhooks

How other systems subscribe people to the center's lists; they don't send e-mail (that's the API). A Form webhook is for a sign-up form on the center's website: whoever signs up is sent the confirmation e-mail, and is subscribed once they click it. A Server webhook is for a system that vouches for the person's consent, such as a booking system whose registration form has a newsletter box: it can subscribe them at once. Only superadmins make Server webhooks. Someone who unsubscribed is sent the e-mail to subscribe again, unless they marked an e-mail as spam or their address bounced.

Setting up a form

Gravity Forms (WordPress), with its Webhooks add-on: in the form's Settings → Webhooks, add one with the Form webhook's address as the Request URL, Request Method POST, Request Format JSON, and Request Body Select Fields, with these keys:

  • email: the e-mail field (required).
  • name, or first_name and last_name: the name fields (optional).
  • consent: a consent checkbox, if the form has one. When it's sent, it has to be ticked.
  • form_id, entry_id, page_url: optional (the {form_id}, {entry_id} and {embed_url} merge tags). They're kept with the subscriber's consent record.
  • attribs.<name>: anything else to keep with someone new, such as attribs.city (optional). An existing subscriber's details aren't changed.
  • tags: tags for someone new, of those the webhook may give (optional; separated by commas).

Turn on the form's spam protection (Gravity Forms' Turnstile or reCAPTCHA field). A bot filling the form would make the center send confirmation e-mails to people who didn't ask for them, and spam complaints follow. The webhook only sees the form's server, not the visitor, so it can only limit how many sign-ups it takes an hour; the admins are told when that's reached.

Any other form or service that can POST JSON or a form with the same fields. A plain HTML form works too, and shows the "check your e-mail" page:


				
The address is secret only in that whoever has it can sign people up, who still have to confirm by e-mail. If it's been misused, give the webhook a new address and update the form. An address gets at most 5 confirmation e-mails a day.
