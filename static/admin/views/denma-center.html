Sign-up webhooks

For the sign-up forms on the center's website. Each webhook has a secret address to give the form, and signs people up to its lists. They're sent the confirmation e-mail, and are subscribed once they click it. Someone who unsubscribed is sent one too, unless they marked an e-mail as spam or their address bounced. Webhooks are saved as they're changed, apart from the Save button above.

Setting up a form

Gravity Forms (WordPress), with its Webhooks add-on: in the form's Settings → Webhooks, add one with the webhook's address as the Request URL, Request Method POST, Request Format JSON, and Request Body Select Fields, with these keys:

  • email: the e-mail field (required).
  • name, or first_name and last_name: the name fields (optional).
  • consent: a consent checkbox, if the form has one. When it's sent, it has to be ticked.
  • form_id, entry_id, page_url: optional (the {form_id}, {entry_id} and {embed_url} merge tags). They're kept with the subscriber's consent record.

Any other form or service that can POST JSON or a form with the same fields. A plain HTML form works too, and shows the "check your e-mail" page:


				
The address is secret only in that whoever has it can sign people up, who still have to confirm by e-mail. If it's been misused, give the webhook a new address and update the form. Each webhook takes at most {{ $d.SignupPerHour }} sign-ups an hour, and an address gets at most 5 confirmation e-mails a day.
