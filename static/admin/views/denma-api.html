Keys

For other systems (a member database, a booking system, a website) that send e-mail through this center. Each system gets a key of its own, which can do one or more of: send transactional e-mails (password resets, receipts, notices), send campaigns to the addresses it gives (without adding them to the center's lists), and read the unsubscribes, complaints and bounces of the people it sends to. Give each key only what its system needs. A sending key sends from its own sender, at most its daily limit of e-mails, under the center's daily cap, with links only to its link domains and the center's, and skips whoever unsubscribed, complained or bounced. If a key may have leaked, delete it as leaked: that cancels its campaigns.

Keys don't subscribe people: sign-up webhooks do (Config → Webhooks). The people a key sends to are added to the center without lists, and with no consent to its mail: they can join its lists only by signing up themselves.
